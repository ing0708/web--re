our story

a calm room, careful hands

775 began with a simple idea — that a manicure should feel like a quiet hour to yourself, not a rushed appointment. We keep our space soft and unhurried, so the only thing you have to think about is the color you'll pick next.

Every set starts with healthy nails. We shape, we prep, and we take the extra minutes most salons skip, because clean lines and lasting wear come from the work you don't see.

the craft

technique you can trust

Our artists are licensed and trained in both classic and modern technique — from a flawless nude gel to fine, detailed art. Tools are sterilized to hospital standards before every visit, no exceptions.

Whether you come in monthly or for one special occasion, you'll leave with nails that look considered, feel comfortable, and hold up to real life.
